## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a Product Engineer at **Foxconn Hon Hai Technology India** in Bengaluru, working on the electronics of a national aerospace project. My focus is high-reliability avionics subsystems, Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) simulation, and automated test systems. My background spans embedded firmware, PCB-level validation, and SMT manufacturing quality, and I've worked across consumer electronics, automotive, research, and aerospace. I'm passionate about catching failures before deployment and turning test data into better designs.
